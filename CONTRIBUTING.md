# Contributing to the Wurm Online Historical Archive

Thank you for helping us preserve the history of Wurm Online. Contributing to this project is an act of **digital archaeology**.

## The Spirit of Contribution

When you upload a log, you are not just "sending a file"—you are **restoring a fragment of a lost timeline**. 

We value every contribution, no matter how small:
- **A few hours of play**: A "Faint Trace" that helps fill a gap.
- **A full month of active logs**: A "Dense Ledger" that provides a clear window into that period.

## Guidelines for Historical Integrity

1. **Upload Raw Logs**: Please upload the raw `.txt` files as generated by the Wurm Online client. Do not edit or trim them, as even "empty" periods provide historical context.
2. **Server Accuracy**: Ensure you select the correct server (NFI, SFI, etc.) to maintain the integrity of the archaeological corpus.
3. **Privacy and Respect**: This archive is for public and semi-public history. While we focus on Trade and Events, we respect player privacy and do not use these logs for surveillance or competitive intelligence.

## Technical Contributions

If you are a developer looking to help build the archive:
- **Preserve the Aesthetic**: Maintain the minimalist, dark, archival design. Avoid adding "modern SaaS" elements like heavy dashboards or gamification.
- **Institutional Stability**: Write code that is simple and maintainable, prioritizing the longevity of the archive over complex features.
- **Respect the Mission**: Every feature should serve the goal of **preservation** and **archival trust**.

---

*Together, we ensure that the history of our world is not lost to time.*

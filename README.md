# Wurm Online Historical Archive — *Archaeology of a Virtual World*

![Wurm Archive Header](https://via.placeholder.com/1000x200/0d0d0d/c5a059?text=WURM+ONLINE+HISTORICAL+ARCHIVE)

## 📜 The Mission

Wurm Online is a world of stories, but those stories are often lost to time. Player logs are the primary source of historical truth, yet they are scattered across thousands of local hard drives, vulnerable to deletion and hardware failure.

The **Wurm Online Historical Archive** is a community-driven initiative dedicated to the immutable preservation of these records. We treat player logs not as mere data, but as **archaeological fragments** of a virtual civilization.

### Phase 0: Archival Preservation
Our current focus is strictly on the **Preservation Layer**. We aim to secure raw historical material before it disappears forever. Interpretation and analytics will be addressed in future phases.

---

## 🏛️ Design Philosophy: *Digital Archaeology*

Unlike modern analytics dashboards or SaaS platforms, this archive is built with an **archival aesthetic** focused on trust, permanence, and calm.

### 1. Meaningful Absence
The gaps in our "Historical Coverage" represent **lost history**. These voids serve as a visual invitation for the community to contribute and help stitch the timeline back together.

### 2. Fragmented Reconstruction
Our visuals reflect the process of **reconstructing fragments**:
- **Weathered Fragments**: UI components use irregular, weathered edges.
- **Restoration Intensity**: Opacity and color intensity reflect data density (Faint Trace vs. Dense Ledger).

### 3. Archival Trust
- **No Accounts Required**: Historical contribution should be frictionless.
- **Client-Side Integrity**: SHA-256 calculation in the browser ensures deduplication and data integrity.
- **Privacy Focused**: We focus on historical records, not player tracking or market intelligence.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Core**: Vanilla JavaScript & CSS (for longevity).
- **Build Tool**: Vite.
- **Backend**: Supabase (PostgreSQL + Storage).
- **Typeface**: EB Garamond (Serif) & Inter (Sans-serif).

---

## 🤝 How to Contribute

1. **Locate your logs**: Find your local Wurm Online log files.
2. **Select your server**: Choose Northern Freedom Isles or Southern Freedom Isles.
3. **Drag & Drop**: Simply drag your logs into the vault on the homepage.
4. **Preserve**: Your contribution becomes a permanent part of the collective history.

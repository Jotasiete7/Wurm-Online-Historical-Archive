# Wurm Online Historical Archive — *Archaeology of a Virtual World*

![Wurm Archive Header](https://via.placeholder.com/1000x200/0d0d0d/c5a059?text=WURM+ONLINE+HISTORICAL+ARCHIVE)

## 📜 The Mission

Wurm Online is a world of stories, but those stories are often lost to time. Player logs are the primary source of historical truth, yet they are scattered across thousands of local hard drives, vulnerable to deletion and hardware failure.

The **Wurm Online Historical Archive** is a community-driven initiative dedicated to the immutable preservation of these records. We treat player logs not as mere data, but as **archaeological fragments** of a virtual civilization.

---

## 🏛️ Design Philosophy: *Digital Archaeology*

Unlike modern analytics dashboards or SaaS platforms, this archive is built with an **archival aesthetic** focused on trust, permanence, and calm.

### 1. Meaningful Absence
The archive does not hide what is missing. The gaps in our "Historical Coverage" are intentional—they represent **lost history**. These voids serve as a visual invitation for the community to contribute and help stitch the timeline back together.

### 2. Fragmented Restoration
Our visualization language reflects the process of **reconstructing manuscripts**. 
- **Weathered Fragments**: Logs are rendered as irregular segments with tattered edges.
- **Intensity of Restoration**: Not all history is equally preserved. We use color intensity to differentiate between a **Faint Trace** (sparse logs) and a **Dense Ledger** (near-complete records).

### 3. Archival Trust
- **No Accounts Required**: Historical contribution should be frictionless.
- **Privacy Focused**: We focus on public and semi-public logs (Trade, Events, etc.). We do not build player profiles or market intelligence tools.
- **Institutional Aesthetic**: Using classic typography (*EB Garamond*) and a minimalist dark composition to create a space for serious preservation.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Core**: Vanilla Javascript & CSS (for longevity and performance).
- **Build Tool**: Vite.
- **Storage & Database**: [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (Immutable log storage).
- **Typeface**: EB Garamond (Serif) & Inter (Sans-serif).

---

## 🤝 How to Contribute

1. **Locate your logs**: Find your local Wurm Online log files (usually in the `logs/` folder of your client).
2. **Select your server**: Choose the appropriate corpus (Northern Freedom Isles or Southern Freedom Isles).
3. **Drag & Drop**: Simply drag your logs into the vault on the homepage.
4. **Stay Anonymous or Be Credited**: You can choose to provide a name for the archive's contributor records or remain an anonymous donor.

---

## 🗺️ Future Direction

The project currently focuses on the **Archival Layer** (Preservation). Future iterations may introduce:
- **Interpretation Layers**: Tools for historians to cross-reference events across different player perspectives.
- **Corpus Search**: Searchable indexes of historical trade movements (without compromising player privacy).
- **Cross-Server Timelines**: Visualizing the migration of players and items between the Isles.
